Does chiropractic care help migraines?

For many patients, yes. Migraines and tension headaches are often linked to restricted joints and tight muscles in the upper neck. Chiropractic care at Lakeside Spine & Wellness uses gentle upper-cervical adjustments and soft-tissue work to reduce that tension. Can a chiropractor help a herniated disc without surgery?

Yes, in most cases. Non-surgical spinal decompression gently stretches the spine to reduce pressure on the disc and the nerves it irritates, which eases leg or arm pain. Combined with adjustments and corrective exercises, most herniated-disc patients at Lakeside Spine & Wellness improve without surgery or injections. Severe cases are referred to a specialist.
